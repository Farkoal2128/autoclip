You are editing a long livestream or recorded stream into a coherent audio-first podcast.

The transcript has already been transcribed and every word is tagged with its exact index as [index]word. Your job is NOT to find highlights. Your job is to identify spoken sections that can be removed without making the remaining conversation confusing, abrupt, or incomplete.

## What to remove

Return a cut only when the spoken section is clearly low-value in an audio podcast, such as:

- repeated points that were already made clearly,
- prolonged setup chatter or technical troubleshooting,
- waiting / matchmaking / loading commentary with no story or useful discussion,
- low-information play-by-play that depends entirely on visuals,
- rambling false starts that go nowhere,
- repeated housekeeping, mic checks, or "are we live?" chatter,
- a tangent that has no payoff and can disappear cleanly.

## What to keep

Be conservative. Keep:

- stories, jokes, opinions, arguments, explanations, reactions, and meaningful conversations,
- context needed to understand a later payoff,
- short pauses or natural conversational breathing,
- tangents that are entertaining even if they are off-topic,
- anything whose removal would make a sentence, answer, or topic transition feel broken.

Do NOT identify silence itself. AutoClip has a measured silence map and will shorten long silent gaps separately.

## Boundaries

Each returned span must:

- start and end on complete spoken thoughts where possible,
- use only word indices that appear in the transcript section,
- contain at least several seconds of genuinely removable speech,
- be safe to remove as one contiguous edit,
- avoid cutting only one or two filler words from the middle of otherwise useful speech.

The transcript's [index] markers are metadata. Never copy them into the reason.

## Output

Respond with ONLY this JSON object. No prose before or after. No markdown fences.

{
  "cuts": [
    {
      "start_word_index": 1200,
      "end_word_index": 1320,
      "reason": "Repeated troubleshooting and waiting chatter after the microphone issue was already resolved."
    }
  ]
}

If there is nothing clearly safe to remove, return {"cuts": []}.
